# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui and Radix primitives; GSAP for selected choreography, Framer Motion for interface transitions, and lucide-react icons. Front-end only.

## Users
PDOA operations teams, public Wi-Fi network integrators and managed service providers, ISPs/backhaul operators, and institutions or venues operating distributed guest Wi-Fi.

## Product Purpose
HotspotOps is an independent service-assurance platform concept that helps operators understand whether public Wi-Fi hotspots deliver usable connectivity, inspect network health, run structured diagnostics, identify likely failure layers, and coordinate incident resolution.

## Positioning
An evidence-led service-assurance layer complementary to existing PDOA, authentication, billing, and access-point controller systems. It tests the intended end-to-end service path and explains likely failure layers; this is a hypothesis for operator validation, not a proven market gap.

## Operating Context
Operators oversee distributed locations and need to distinguish failures across wireless association, DHCP, gateway, DNS, TCP/TLS, HTTP/internet, and captive portal flows. Future architecture may use a local/edge probe; no probe is currently deployed.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The website MVP includes a public marketing route and interactive browser-based demo with synthetic hotspot inventory, overview, scenario diagnostics, incidents, explainable statistical anomaly insights, deterministic scenario-grounded Copilot preview, and illustrative reports. All values and locations are fictional. No live network tests, PM-WANI/PDOA integrations, accounts, production telemetry, external LLM, trained ML, or remediation are present. Diagnostics own test states; assistant responses explain only included evidence and identify unsupported questions. Local demo changes reset on refresh.

## Brand Commitments
Use HotspotOps consistently. Descriptor: Public Wi-Fi Service Assurance & Operations Platform. Tagline: “Know which hotspots work. Know why the others fail.” Independent concept, not government-affiliated or endorsed. Do not use government emblems, seals, official PM-WANI assets, or unsupported certification/market claims. Tone: precise, calm, technical, trustworthy.

## Evidence on Hand
Product brief and builder instructions supplied by the user. No real customer evidence, testimonials, logos, operational measurements, or production integrations; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- Show evidence before diagnosis.
- Treat upstream-dependent stages as blocked or not run when prerequisites fail.
- Communicate uncertainty and limits plainly.
- Keep the demo deterministic and synthetic.
- Complement operators’ existing network systems.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive web experience, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and readable contrast are required.
